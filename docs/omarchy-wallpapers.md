## Everlabs logo

The Everlabs symbol was extracted from the user-supplied `everlabs-trainual-hackathon-logo.svg`, preserving its five path elements and original white faces. The surrounding badge and Trainual symbol are omitted.

Editable vector: [everlabs-mark.svg](../assets/everlabs-mark.svg).
App resource: [everlabs-mark.pdf](../Sources/FocusDeck/Resources/Branding/everlabs-mark.pdf), rendered as vector PDF with librsvg.
The bottom-left link shares the task controls’ visibility and fade timing, highlights on direct hover, and opens https://everlabs.com. Normal opacity is 38%; direct hover raises it to 75%.

Gallery assigns one still image per task; there is no timed wallpaper rotation. A selected fixed wallpaper remains still even when the task changes.
