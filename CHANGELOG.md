# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Two ways to capture a task for Today, with all buttons in one row: **Add to Today** makes it next without switching, and **Add to Today & focus** switches immediately.
- The explicit next-task choice survives synchronization and app restarts; tasks no longer in Today are skipped.

## 1.4.0 – 2026-09-17

### Added

- Upcoming calendar events above Current Focus, with a live countdown and local start time.
- Calendar connection and selection in Settings, using accounts connected to Apple Calendar.
- Adjustable reminder lead time from 1 to 120 minutes, defaulting to 10 minutes.
- Quiet, dim reminders that become white with a border during the final minute, then briefly show “Starting now”.
- Automatic filtering of all-day, cancelled, and declined events.

### Changed

- Simplified the Spotify hover menu: removed volume controls, moved Next to the upper-right, and made the background 80% opaque.

### Getting started

Open **Settings → Calendar → Connect Calendar** and allow calendar access. Select your calendars and preferred lead time. Google and Outlook accounts must first be connected to Apple Calendar through macOS Internet Accounts.

Download and replace the previous app to update. Existing tasks and preferences are kept. There is no automatic updater.

## 1.3.0 – 2026-09-15

- First public preview for macOS 13+, with Apple silicon and Intel support.
- Todoist Today task creation, completion, and next-task selection.
- Spotify playback controls and album artwork.
- Six quiet wallpapers, one image per task, and an optional always-on-top window.
